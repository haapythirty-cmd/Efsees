Frequently Asked Questions

Is Kwara Life free?

Yes. You can create an account and play the full city for free. Optional virtual-naira top-ups are available via Paystack if you want extra spending power. Nothing essential is locked behind payment.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs entirely in your browser on desktop or mobile.

Does my progress save?

Yes. When you are logged in, your character, money, needs, home, skills and relationships are saved on the server. You can continue on any device.

Can I chat with other players?

Yes. There is a live global chat and location-based messages so you can talk to people in the same place.

Is this affiliated with Lagos Life?

No. Kwara Life is an independent project inspired by the life-sim genre and the culture of Kwara State. It is not affiliated with the developers of Lagos Life.

Age requirement?

You must be 18 or older to register and play.
